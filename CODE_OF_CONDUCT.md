# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — contributors and maintainers — pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Using the project to scan or attack systems without authorization, or encouraging
  others to do so.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to
the project maintainer through a confidential report at
<https://github.com/streetshadows/website-audit/security/advisories/new>. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainer will respect the
privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct may be removed from the
project team.
